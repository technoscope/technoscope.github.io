1. Introduction

Welcome to Brain Boost (“the Game”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), developed by OneStopSolutions. Brain Boost is a collection of puzzle games designed to challenge and sharpen your mind through handcrafted levels across beautifully themed worlds.

We are dedicated to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use Brain Boost, how it is used, and your rights in relation to it.

By downloading or playing Brain Boost, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Brain Boost does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) directly. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, or account credentials.

The following non-personal game data is stored locally on your device only via standard storage:

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

3.1 Google AdMob (Advertising)

Brain Boost displays rewarded video advertisements powered by Google AdMob. Watching a rewarded ad is entirely voluntary — players choose to view an ad to receive bonus game rewards (such as an extra life).

Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain technical data to serve advertisements, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID / GAID)
  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version
  • IP address (used to determine general country/region location)
  • Ad interaction metrics (views, clicks, impressions)

This data is collected and processed independently by Google LLC in accordance with their privacy policies. OneStopSolutions does not receive or store your personal identifiers.

For more details, see:
• Google Privacy Policy
• Google AdMob Partner Policies

How to opt out: Android users can reset or delete their Advertising ID and opt out of personalized ads at any time through:
Settings → Google → Ads → Reset Advertising ID / Delete Advertising ID.

3.2 Google Play In-App Review

Brain Boost uses the official Google Play In-App Review API to let users rate and review the game directly within the app. This process is managed entirely by the Google Play Store app on your device. We do not receive or store any personal data through this feature.

5. Children's Privacy

Brain Boost is a family-friendly puzzle game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and wish to disable personalized advertising on your child's device, you can do so through the device's Google Ads settings.

6. Data Security

Because all game progress is kept locally on your personal device, your data is protected by your device's own native security features (encryption, screen locks, etc.). We do not host or manage external servers containing your personal data.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

All locally stored game progress remains on your device until you:

  • Uninstall the Game: Android automatically purges all locally stored data upon uninstallation.
  • Clear App Data: You can clear all data manually via Settings → Apps → Brain Boost → Storage → Clear Data.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as GDPR in Europe or CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have statutory rights regarding data collection. Because OneStopSolutions does not collect or store personal data on remote servers, your primary control is directly on your device (clearing data, resetting ad ID).

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any revisions will be reflected by the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page. We recommend reviewing this policy from time to time.
