Pro Teleprompter for Android

Published by Technoscope / OneStop Solutions

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Scripts, files and settings

Pro Teleprompter uses the scripts you type or import to show your words as you rehearse and record. Scripts, reading progress, presets, settings and imported font copies are stored in the app's local storage. The app does not require an account or operate a developer-run sync service.

When you import or export a file, Android opens a file picker so you can choose the source or destination. If you choose a cloud file provider or a shared location, that provider handles the file under its own terms. Android or your device provider may back up or transfer app data according to your settings.

Camera and microphone

When you use the built-in camera, the app uses camera access for the preview and recording. Microphone access is used for recording audio, showing the microphone level, and voice pacing when you turn it on. Recordings are saved to device storage and can be saved to your gallery. The app does not upload your recordings to the developer.

Voice pacing uses Android's on-device speech recognizer. The app uses recognized words temporarily to move the script. It does not save transcripts or send speech audio to a server. Voice pacing requires a supported on-device recognizer and language. If local recognition is unavailable, you can use manual scrolling.

Floating overlay and remote buttons

Display over other apps permission lets the app show your script in a floating window over compatible apps. The window displays the script you select. Pro Teleprompter does not use the overlay permission to read the other app's screen.

If you enable Teleprompter overlay remote in Android Accessibility settings, Android can deliver hardware key events to the app while the overlay is open. The service handles supported media buttons to control play, pause, paragraph navigation and speed. It does not read screen content or store or send key input. Other keys are passed through. You can disable the service in Android Accessibility settings.

Retention, deletion and permissions

Scripts are kept until you delete them or clear the app's storage. Use Android Settings to clear local app data or revoke permissions. Use your gallery or file manager to delete recordings and exports separately, including copies you backed up. Uninstalling the app clears its private local storage. Files in shared storage, backups or external locations may remain.
