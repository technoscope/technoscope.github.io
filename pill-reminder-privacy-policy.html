Privacy Policy

Pill Reminder & Med Tracker

App Name: Pill Reminder & Med Tracker
Package Name: com.ossol.pill.reminder.tracker
Developer: OneStop Solutions
Effective Date: 5 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how OneStop Solutions ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Pill Reminder & Med Tracker mobile application for Android (the "App"). The App may appear on Google Play under a name that adds a brand word to "Pill Reminder"; this policy applies to it under any such name.

We designed the App with privacy in mind. You do not need to create an account. Your medications, dose history and health journal stay on your device. We do not sell your personal information. This policy explains what is and is not collected, so you can make informed decisions.

Summary:
  • Your medications, schedules, dose history, profiles and health journal entries are stored only on your device. We never receive them, and no advertising, analytics or purchase service receives them.
  • The App uses Google services (AdMob for ads, Firebase for analytics, crash reports and remote settings) and RevenueCat with Google Play Billing for purchases. These collect limited technical data, such as device identifiers, app usage and crash information, as described in Section 2.
  • Health information leaves your device only if you export a backup file or share a PDF report.

1. Information Stored Only on Your Device

1.1 Medication and dose information

When you use the App, you enter information such as medication names, dosages, form (for example, tablet or drops), schedules, stock counts, instructions and notes. The App records each dose as taken, skipped (with an optional reason, such as "side effects"), snoozed or missed, and the time you actually took it.

1.2 Profiles for family members and pets

You can keep separate medication lists for other people (for example, a child or a parent) or for pets. A profile holds a name you choose and that profile's medications and history. If you enter information about another person, please make sure you are allowed to do so. Profiles are stored only on your device.

1.3 Health journal

If you use the optional health journal, you can record blood pressure and pulse, blood glucose, weight, mood and pain readings, with optional notes. The App stores and shows these values exactly as you enter them. It does not interpret them or give advice.

1.4 Settings

The App stores your settings on your device, for example your reminder repeat interval, units, theme colour, the alarm sound you chose for a medication, and whether app lock is on.

All of the information in Section 1 is stored only on your device in a local database. It is not uploaded to our servers (we do not run any), and it is not sent to any third-party service. The App also excludes this database and its settings from Android's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so it is not copied to your Google account backup. If you uninstall the App or clear its storage, this information is permanently deleted unless you have exported a backup file (Section 4.1).

1.5 Reminders on your lock screen

To make sure you notice a dose, reminder notifications and the full-screen alarm can show the medication name and dose on your lock screen, where anyone holding your phone can see them. You can change how notifications appear on the lock screen in your device's notification settings.

2. Information Collected by Third-Party Services

The App includes the following third-party services. They collect limited technical information automatically. None of them receives your medication, dose, profile or health journal information.

2.1 Google AdMob (advertising) and consent

If you have not purchased the upgrade, the App shows advertisements from Google AdMob in a banner on some screens. Ads are never shown on the alarm screen, on the lock screen or in reminder notifications.

According to Google, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares your device's IP address (which may be used to estimate the general location of your device), interactions with the app and ads (such as app launches, taps and video views), diagnostic information about the performance of the app and the SDK, and device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.

Consent: Before any ad is requested, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check whether a privacy or consent message must be shown in your region (for example, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland). If a message is shown, the App requests ads only as your choices allow. When the App offers it, you can change your choices later in Settings > Privacy options.

Ad personalisation: By default, the App asks Google to show non-personalised ads. We may change this setting in the future; if we do, your consent choices still apply. We never pass health or medication information to the advertising service, and we do not use it to target ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (on most devices: Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).

2.2 Google Firebase Analytics and measuring our advertising

We use Firebase Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the App is used (for example, how many people open it and which screens are viewed), and to measure the advertising campaigns we run to promote the App, such as Google Ads (for example, whether people who installed the App from an ad later bought the upgrade). According to Google, it collects screen views and sessions, in-app purchase and subscription events (product, name and price), an app-instance identifier, the Android advertising ID, and an approximate location derived from your device's IP address, together with device and app information such as device model, operating system version, app version and language. Because the App uses Google AdMob, Analytics also records ad impressions and the ad revenue they earn.

No health data: We do not send any events about your medications, doses or health to Analytics or to Google Ads, and we do not use App data to build advertising audiences (for example, remarketing or similar-audience lists).

Consent: Analytics starts with all storage of identifiers turned off. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, it stays off unless you agree in Google's consent message, and it follows the choices you make there (you can change them later in Settings > Privacy options). In other regions it is turned on once the App has checked that this consent is not required or has been given. Analytics data is never used to personalise ads. While storage is off, Google may still receive limited measurements without identifiers.

2.3 Google Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to receive reports when the App crashes. According to Google, a crash report includes the stack trace (which code was running), the state of the App at the time, device information such as model and operating system version, and a Crashlytics installation identifier. We do not add your medication or health information to crash reports.

2.4 Google Firebase Remote Config

Each time the App starts, it downloads settings from Firebase Remote Config. These settings control, for example, whether ads are shown, how often, and when the App may suggest the upgrade. According to Google, Remote Config collects your country, language, time zone, operating system and SDK versions, the App's identifiers and a Firebase installation identifier, in order to deliver the right settings. Remote Config does not receive your medication or health information.

2.5 RevenueCat and Google Play Billing (purchases)

The App offers an optional upgrade ("Pro") as a subscription or a one-time purchase. Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Google, as the payment processor, handles your payment information under Google's Privacy Policy. We never receive your card details.

We use RevenueCat to check purchases with Google Play and to know whether your upgrade is active. RevenueCat receives your purchase history (products, transaction details and their status) linked to an anonymous identifier that RevenueCat creates. We do not send RevenueCat your name, email address or any medication or health information. RevenueCat also provides the upgrade screen inside the App.

You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in Google Play (Google Play subscriptions) or from Settings > Manage subscription in the App.

3. Permissions the App Uses

  • Notifications: to show dose reminders and refill alerts.
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms): to ring at the exact time you set, even when the device is idle.
  • Full-screen notifications: to show the dose alarm over the lock screen, like an alarm clock.
  • Run at startup: to restore your reminders after the device restarts.
  • Wake lock and vibrate: to make the alarm audible and noticeable when it rings.
  • Biometric / fingerprint hardware: for the optional app lock (Section 4.3).
  • Internet and network state: used only by the third-party services in Section 2.
  • Advertising ID and related advertising/attribution APIs: used by Google AdMob and Firebase Analytics as described in Section 2.
  • Basic phone state: requested by the Google Mobile Ads SDK; the App's own features do not use it.
  • Google Play Billing and Play install referrer: for purchases (Section 2.5) and analytics (Section 2.2).

The App does not request your location, camera, contacts, calendar, SMS, microphone or files permission.

4. Features Where You Decide to Move Your Data

4.1 Backup export and import

In Settings > Your data you can export a backup file containing your medications, dose history, profiles and health journal, and import it again, for example on a new phone. The file is created only when you tap Export, and it is saved wherever you choose in the Android file picker (which may include a cloud storage app you have set up on your phone). The backup file is not encrypted. Anyone who can open the file can read the health information in it. You control where it is stored and who can access it. We never receive a copy.

4.2 Doctor report (PDF)

With the upgrade, you can create a PDF report of a profile's medications and dose history (and, if you choose, health journal entries) to share with your doctor. The report is created on your device. When you tap Share, Android shows the apps you can send it to, and the report goes only to the app and the people you choose; you can also save it as a file. A temporary copy is kept in the App's private storage area. It is deleted when you create the next report or, once it is more than an hour old, the next time you open the report screen; Android may also clear it sooner. We never receive a copy.

4.3 App lock

With the upgrade, you can lock the App with your fingerprint, face or your device's screen lock (PIN, pattern or password). The check is performed by Android. The App never receives or stores your fingerprint, face data or screen-lock code; Android only tells the App whether the check succeeded. Dose alarms keep working while the App is locked.

5. How We Use Information

We use the information described in Section 2 to:

  • show ads to users who have not purchased the upgrade, and respect their consent choices;
  • verify purchases and unlock the upgrade;
  • control App settings remotely (for example, ad frequency or switching a feature off if it misbehaves);
  • diagnose and fix crashes and bugs;
  • understand, in aggregate, how the App is used so we can improve it;
  • measure and improve our own advertising campaigns that promote the App (for example, purchases and ad revenue that follow an install from an ad), without using any health information;
  • prevent fraud and abuse, and meet legal obligations.

We do not use your medication, dose, profile or health journal information for any of these purposes, because that information never leaves your device unless you export or share it yourself.

6. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only as follows:

  • Google (AdMob): the advertising data in Section 2.1 is collected and shared by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which may work with advertising partners, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.
  • Service providers that process data on our behalf: Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config) and RevenueCat.
  • Google Ads: when we run ads for the App, purchase and ad-revenue events and the related app and advertising identifiers from Section 2.2 are used by Google to measure and optimise those ads, as your consent choices allow.
  • Google Play: which processes payments.
  • Legal authorities: if required by law, subpoena, court order or other legal process, or to protect against fraud, abuse or harm.
  • A successor: in the event of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to equivalent privacy protections.

7. Data Retention

  • Information on your device (Section 1) stays until you delete it, clear the App's storage or uninstall the App. Deleting a profile deletes its medications, history and journal entries.
  • Backup files and shared reports stay wherever you put them until you delete them.
  • Firebase Crashlytics keeps crash data for 90 days before starting to delete it.
  • Firebase installation identifiers are kept until they are deleted on request.
  • Firebase Analytics data is kept according to the retention setting of our Google Analytics property.
  • Google AdMob and RevenueCat keep data according to their own policies.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children. Profiles for children are meant to be created and managed by a parent or carer on their own device. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service in the App, please contact us and we will help to have it deleted.

10. Data Security

Information on your device is protected by your device's security, and you can add the optional app lock. The App blocks unencrypted network traffic; the third-party services in Section 2 send data over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Exported backup files are not encrypted (Section 4.1). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

11. International Data Transfers
