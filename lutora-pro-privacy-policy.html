Core Privacy Guarantee:

Lutora Pro is engineered around 100% on-device video processing. Your video footage, imported 3D .cube LUT files, color grades, and exported videos never leave your phone. We do not operate media processing servers, do not require user accounts, and never upload, view, or sell your video content.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Lutora Pro (the "App"), developed by Technoscope / OneStop Solutions ("we", "us", or "our"), distributed via Google Play Store (Package Name: com.onestop.lutora • Google Play Store Listing).

We recognize the importance of your creative privacy. Whether you are a professional colorist, mobile videographer, or casual content creator, your media files and color styling choices remain exclusively yours. This document explains what technical data the App interacts with, why specific Android device permissions are required, and your rights regarding your data.

2. On-Device Processing & Media Handling

Lutora Pro processes all video clips, color grades, and LUT transformations locally using your device's hardware:

  • ✓ Local Video Pipeline: Video frames are decoded via Android's native MediaCodec, processed through an OpenGL ES 3.0 graphics pipeline with 16-bit float intermediates, and re-encoded directly to your device storage.
  • ✓ 3D LUT Imports: User-imported .cube 3D LUT files and ZIP LUT packs are parsed and cached locally in private application storage.
  • ✓ Zero Remote Media Uploads: The App does not send your videos, thumbnails, audio streams, or file names to external servers.
  • ✓ No Account Registration: You do not need to register an account, sign in, or provide personal contact information to use Lutora Pro.

3. Android Permissions and Why They Are Needed

The App requests standard Android system permissions strictly necessary to perform color grading and batch export:

Permission Technical Reason & Usage
Photos and Videos / Media Access
READ_MEDIA_VIDEO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE		 Enables you to select video clips from your gallery/storage for color grading and allows saving graded MP4 exports to your device's public video storage.
Foreground Service
FOREGROUND_SERVICE, MEDIA_PROCESSING, DATA_SYNC		 Required on Android 14+ (API 34+) to complete intensive batch video rendering jobs in the background without being terminated prematurely by OS memory management when you navigate away.
Notifications
POST_NOTIFICATIONS		 Displays real-time export progress bars in your notification tray during batch rendering and notifies you when the export successfully completes. No marketing or push advertisements are sent.
Wake Lock
WAKE_LOCK		 Prevents the device CPU and GPU from sleeping while an active hardware video encoding batch is in progress.
Internet Access
INTERNET		 Enables Firebase Remote Config parameter sync, optional pseudonymous crash/usage analytics (if enabled), and Google Play In-App Billing verification. Media is never uploaded over the internet.

4. Analytics, Remote Config, and Telemetry

Lutora Pro utilizes standard Google Firebase developer tools to optimize app performance and maintain reliability across varied Android hardware:

Google Firebase Analytics (Google LLC)

Firebase Analytics provides aggregated, pseudonymous statistics (e.g., app launches, general feature usage, render durations, device hardware specifications, OS version). It does not collect user names, phone numbers, email addresses, video contents, or file paths.

User Control & Opt-Out:
You can disable analytics reporting at any time. Inside the App, go to Settings › Privacy › Share usage analytics and turn the toggle off. Your preference is saved immediately and disables analytics collection.

Google Firebase Remote Config (Google LLC)

Remote Config allows us to deploy performance tuning parameters, default sample clip settings, and critical bug fix configurations without requiring an entire app update. Remote Config does not transmit personal data.

5. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Lutora Pro offers an optional Pro version upgrade allowing batch processing workflows and advanced features:

  • All purchases are conducted exclusively through Google Play In-App Billing.
  • We do not receive, process, or store your credit card numbers, bank details, or billing address. All payment information is securely processed by Google.
  • Purchase entitlements are verified securely with the Google Play Store client to restore Pro features across your devices linked to your Google Account.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Lutora Pro does not store personal profiles, video footage, or grade metadata on cloud databases:

  • All project records, clip queues, custom looks, and imported LUTs are stored in a sandboxed local SQLite database (lutora.db) on your device.
  • Clearing application data via Android Settings or uninstalling the App permanently deletes all local application state and cached LUTs immediately.

8. Global Compliance (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), users have specific rights regarding their personal data:

  • Right to Access & Portability: Since we do not maintain accounts or server-side user databases, all user data exists directly on your device.
  • Right to Erasure: You can delete all local data at any time by clearing app cache/storage or uninstalling the App.
  • No Sale of Personal Information: We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or media files to data brokers or third parties.
