1. Your Videos, Photos, Audio, and AI Voiceover

  • Photo & Video Selection: Media files are imported using Android's system photo picker (PickVisualMedia). This ensures EditLine only accesses the specific photos and videos you explicitly choose, without requiring broad access to your entire media library.
  • On-Device Editing & Rendering: Trimming, splitting, speed changes, video reversing, transitions, filters, color adjustments, audio detachment, and video exporting happen entirely on your device using hardware-accelerated Media3 and MediaCodec pipelines.
  • AI Voiceover (Text-to-Speech): When you use the AI Voiceover feature, speech synthesis is performed 100% locally on your device by an embedded on-device model (Kokoro TTS). Text entered for speech synthesis is processed locally and is never transmitted over the internet to any external cloud service.
  • Audio Recordings & Detached Audio: Voiceover recordings made via the microphone and audio detached from video clips are stored strictly in the app's private sandbox storage on your device.
  • Saving & Sharing: Rendered videos are saved directly to your device gallery (Movies › EditLine). When you choose to share a video via the system share sheet, the video file is transferred directly to the application you select, governed by that application's privacy policy.
  • Local Project Storage: Project metadata, undo/redo history, and settings are stored locally on your device. You can erase this data at any time by deleting individual projects within the app, clearing app storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version displays banner, interstitial, app-open, and rewarded ads provided by Google AdMob. When requesting an advertisement, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares certain information with Google:

  • Device Identifiers: Mobile Advertising ID (AAID) and App Set ID.
  • Approximate Location: Coarse geographic location derived from your device's IP address.
  • App Interactions: Ad impressions, clicks, views, and app launch events.
  • SDK Diagnostics: Crash logs and technical performance metrics.

Google uses this information to deliver, personalize, and measure advertisements, as well as to detect and prevent fraud. For full details on how Google handles advertising data, review How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

EditLine Pro: Upgrading to Pro completely removes all advertisements.

4. Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

EditLine utilizes Google Analytics for Firebase (on Android) to help us understand how users engage with the app, identify technical issues, and improve app stability. Firebase Analytics collects:

  • App Usage Metrics: App launches, session durations, screen views, and engagement with editing features (e.g., export started, tool selected).
  • Device & Technical Information: Device model, operating system version, app version code, language setting, and general device hardware specs.
  • Approximate Geographic Data: City or country-level location derived from IP address.

Firebase Analytics data is pseudonymous and aggregated. We do not transmit personal names, email addresses, phone numbers, or user video/audio content to Firebase. For more details on Firebase data security and privacy, review Firebase Privacy and Security and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Consent and Privacy Choices

  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland: In compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and ePrivacy Directive, the app presents a Google-certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog on first launch. You can review or modify your consent choices at any time directly in the app under Settings › Privacy choices.
  • Everywhere: You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time in your device settings under Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads). Deleting the Advertising ID prevents advertising networks from associating ad interactions with your device.

6. Purchases & Subscriptions (Google Play Billing)

EditLine Pro (available as monthly, yearly, or lifetime plans) is sold exclusively through Google Play In-App Billing.

  • Payment Processing: All transactions are handled securely by Google Play. We do not collect, process, or store your payment card numbers, banking details, or billing addresses.
  • Subscription Management: Google Play returns a secure purchase token confirming your Pro status. The app queries Google Play on launch to maintain active subscription entitlements.
  • Cancellations & Refunds: You can manage, change, or cancel subscriptions at any time through Google Play Store › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions, or via the Manage subscription button in the app.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

We do not operate external user database servers, and we do not store your media files remotely. All projects, export history, and local preferences remain in the app's sandboxed storage on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Analytics and advertising data collected by Google are retained according to Google's standard retention schedules. You can manage data associated with your Google account at myaccount.google.com.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or California Consumer Privacy Act / CPRA):

  • You have the right to request access to or deletion of your personal data.
  • Because we do not store personal information or account profiles on developer servers, uninstalling the app or clearing app storage deletes all locally stored data immediately.
  • We do not sell or share personal information for monetary value.
  • To exercise rights regarding data processed by Google services (AdMob, Firebase, Google Play), please consult Google's privacy portal.

9. Children's Privacy

EditLine is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us immediately.

10. Security

All communications between the app and third-party SDKs (Google AdMob, Firebase, Google Play) are encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS). Your local files are protected by Android's application sandboxing architecture.
