1. Introduction

Welcome to ClipCut: Video Editor & Maker (“the App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), developed by OneStopSolutions. We provide high-performance, mobile video editing tools that allow users to trim, merge, color grade, add audio, and produce high-resolution videos for social media platforms such as TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through compliance with this Privacy Policy and applicable data protection regulations including Google Play Developer Policies, GDPR, and CCPA/CPRA.

2. User Media & On-Device Processing (No Cloud Uploads)

ClipCut is engineered as an offline-first video editor. All media manipulations—including cutting, trimming, applying 3D LUT filters, rendering kinetic text overlays, adjusting speed curves, and exporting lossless 4K 60FPS video—take place locally using your device's built-in hardware acceleration and local storage.

  • ✓ Your media is private: We do not have access to your personal videos, photos, or voice recordings.
  • ✓ No external uploads: Media files selected from your gallery are loaded directly into the app's local memory and are never transmitted to our servers or any third-party infrastructure.
  • ✓ Export destination: Exported MP4 videos and GIFs are saved directly into your device's local photo/video gallery under your direct control.

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

To deliver core video editing features, ClipCut requests specific Android operating system permissions. You can grant or revoke these permissions at any time via your device settings:

Permission Technical Identifier Usage Purpose
Videos Access READ_MEDIA_VIDEO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Allows you to select and import video clips into the editor timeline.
Audio Access READ_MEDIA_AUDIO Allows you to pick background music and sound tracks stored on your device.
Photos Access READ_MEDIA_IMAGES Allows you to select photos for B-roll overlays or Picture-in-Picture (PIP) layers.
Microphone RECORD_AUDIO Strictly used when you tap the "Record Voiceover" button to record audio directly onto the timeline. Audio is saved locally only.
Camera (Optional) CAMERA Used only if you choose to record video footage directly from within the app.
Internet & Network INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Used solely for displaying advertisements via Google AdMob and checking internet connectivity.
In-App Purchases com.android.vending.BILLING Processes premium subscription or ad-removal purchases securely through Google Play Billing.

6. User Data Rights & Data Retention

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA)), you hold specific privacy rights:

  • Right to Deletion: Because all project files, draft edits, and media clips are stored locally on your device, you have complete autonomy to delete them at any time by clearing the app's cache/data or uninstalling the app.
  • Right to Restrict or Revoke Permissions: You may toggle camera, microphone, or storage permissions on or off at any moment in your Android device's Settings > Apps > ClipCut > Permissions.
  • Personalized Ads Opt-Out: You can opt out of personalized interest-based advertising on your Android device by visiting Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID.
  • No Sale of Data: We do NOT sell, trade, or rent any user data to third parties.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical measures to safeguard your app interactions. Since user project files and video exports reside locally within the application sandbox on your device, your files are protected by Android's native sandboxing security model.
